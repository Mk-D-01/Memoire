Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Mémoire is a local-first memory book for organizing personal photos. This policy explains what information the application handles and where it is stored.

Information stored locally

Photos, captions, layout preferences, and session metadata that you add to Mémoire are stored in your browser using IndexedDB, localStorage, or sessionStorage. Mémoire does not send this local content to the app operator by default.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Mémoire requests access to the Drive permissions shown in Google's consent screen. Photos and session backups are transferred directly between your browser and Google Drive. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy. You can revoke access from your Google Account security settings.

Collaborative features

If collaborative lobbies are enabled for your deployment, the collaboration service may process your chosen display name, guest identifier, lobby membership, and photos shared in a lobby. Do not upload content or personal information that you do not want collaborators to see.

Analytics and advertising

Mémoire does not intentionally use advertising trackers, analytics pixels, or third-party advertising cookies.
